 About the challenge 
 Hack The Arts is a global, online hackathon inviting participants to explore how technology can 
 unlock entirely new forms of creative expression. The challenge is centered on the theme: 
 “Create art that couldn’t exist without technology.” Rather than using code as a tool to replicate 
 traditional art, participants are encouraged to build projects that define new artistic mediums 
 through interactivity, computation, and intelligent systems. 
 Submissions may include generative art, interactive experiences, creative coding tools, 
 AI-assisted artistic workflows, or entirely new forms of digital expression. Strong projects will go 
 beyond static outputs, emphasizing systems that respond, evolve, or engage users in 
 meaningful ways. Participants are encouraged to experiment, take creative risks, and think 
 critically about how technology shapes the way art is created and experienced. 
 Hack The Arts is open to beginners and experienced builders alike. Whether you are a 
 developer, designer, or artist, the goal is to learn, build, and push the boundaries of what art can 
 be in a digital world. 
 Get started 
 1.  Register for the hackathon on Devpost and join the  Discord server  . 
 2.  Form a team of up to four participants or work individually. 
 3.  Brainstorm ideas that align with the theme and explore creative directions. 
 4.  Start building during the official Build Period using any tools, frameworks, or 
 technologies. 
 5.  Attend optional workshops, mentorship sessions, and community events for guidance 
 and inspiration. 
 6.  Submit your project before the deadline, including a demo, source code, and project 
 description. 
 If you are unsure where to begin, start by thinking about how art could respond to something 
 dynamic, such as sound, movement, data, or user interaction, and build from there. 
 Requirements 
 What to Build 
 Build a project that aligns with the theme: “Create art that couldn’t exist without technology.” 
 Your project should combine code and creativity to produce an interactive, generative, or 
 computational form of art. This may include, but is not limited to, creative coding tools, 
 AI-assisted artistic systems, immersive experiences, data-driven visuals, or experimental digital 
 mediums. Projects should emphasize interactivity, evolution, or user engagement rather than 
 static outputs. All work must be created substantially during the Build Period. 
 What to Submit 
 Each submission must include the following: 
 ●  A working project demo, provided as a live link or a recorded video 
 ●  A publicly accessible source code repository 
 ●  A written project description explaining the concept, implementation, and technologies 
 used 
 ●  Clear attribution for any third-party assets, libraries, or APIs used 
 Submissions must be complete and submitted before the deadline. Incomplete, inaccessible, or 
 non-compliant submissions may be disqualified at the organizer’s discretion.

 Project and Submission Requirements 
 Projects must be created during the Build Period. Use of pre-existing code, libraries, 
 frameworks, and APIs is permitted, provided such use is disclosed and properly credited. Each 
 submission must include a working demo (live link or video), a publicly accessible source code 
 repository, and a written project description outlining the concept, implementation, and 
 technologies used. Projects must align with the theme “Create art that couldn’t exist without 
 technology.” Late, incomplete, or non-compliant submissions may be disqualified at the 
 organizer’s discretion. 
 Prizes 
 Prizes will be awarded in categories including, but not limited to, Best Overall Project, Crowd 
 Favorite, Most Unique, Best Interactive Experience, and Best Beginner Project. Prizes may 
 include cash awards, sponsor-provided products or credits, and/or non-monetary recognition. All 
 prizes are non-transferable and may be substituted at the organizer’s discretion. Winners are 
 responsible for any applicable taxes and fees. 
 Judging Criteria and Winner Selection 
 Eligible submissions will be evaluated by a panel of judges based on the following criteria: 
 Creativity and Originality (30%), Use of Technology (25%), Interactivity and Experience (20%), 
 Execution (15%), and Theme Alignment (10%). Judging decisions are final and binding. The 
 organizer reserves the right not to award a prize in any category if, in its sole discretion, no 
 eligible submission meets the required standards.
